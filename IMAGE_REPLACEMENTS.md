# Image replacements

All photographs on the site are Unsplash placeholders. Replace them with your own files (for example under `public/photos/`) and update the URLs in `lib/site.ts`.

| Location | Current role | Replace with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero (`site.hero`) | Full-bleed landing image | Your strongest signature photograph |
| Work `w1` — Morning window | Portrait gallery tile | A favorite natural-light portrait |
| Work `w2` — Coast line | Landscape gallery tile | A landscape or travel frame you return to |
| Work `w3` — Studio calm | Portrait gallery tile | A clean studio or controlled-light portrait |
| Work `w4` — City hush | Street gallery tile | A street or documentary moment |
| Work `w5` — Soft room | Interior gallery tile | An interior or still-life study |
| Work `w6` — Open field | Landscape gallery tile | A wide outdoor frame with atmosphere |

Also update each item's `title`, `category`, and `alt` text when you swap files.
